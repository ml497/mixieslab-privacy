Keeping your progress

The app starts with a guest game. To keep your progress when you change or reset your device, open Stats → Overview and choose Connect account to link the game with Sign in with Apple or Google. On a new device, connect the same account to recover your game.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Stats → Overview → Manage account → Delete Account. This removes your saved game data and the player account. If you cannot use the app, email the address above and ask for your account to be deleted.
