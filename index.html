Privacy Policy

Mixie's Bubble Lab · Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mixie's Bubble Lab app ("the app") handles, why, and what choices you have. The app is developed and operated by Matthias Lenz ("I", "me").

Summary

  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your information is not sold and is not shared for marketing.
  • You can play as a guest. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Information the app handles

Guest account. When you start the app, it creates an anonymous player account with a random identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name or email address.

Optional sign-in. If you choose to link your account with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, the app receives an account identifier from that provider and, depending on what the provider shares, your email address. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address. The app never sees your Apple or Google password.

Game data. To save your progress and restore it on another device, the app stores your game saves, your in-game currency balance, the books you have unlocked, and your daily challenge progress on its servers.

Security and technical data. The app uses device and app integrity checks (Apple App Attest and Firebase App Check) to protect the service from abuse. As with any online service, the servers process your IP address and basic request information, which may be kept in short-lived server logs.

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

How the information is used

  • To provide the game, save your progress, and restore it across devices.
  • To keep track of your in-game currency and unlocked books.
  • To let you sign in and to recover your account.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, the legal bases are the performance of the contract with you (providing the game and your account) and legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and working).

Service providers

The app relies on the following providers, which process data on my behalf or as part of the sign-in you choose:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, Google Cloud hosting and storage, Google Sign-In) – Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Attest) – Apple Privacy Policy

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long information is kept

Your account and game data are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the active data is removed. Backup and recovery copies held by the hosting provider expire automatically after a limited period.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Stats → Overview → Manage account → Delete Account. This works for guest accounts and for accounts linked with Apple or Google. Deletion removes your saved game data, in-game currency, unlocked books, and the player account. It does not delete your Apple or Google account itself.

If you cannot use the app, you can also request deletion using the contact details below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise these rights, use the contact details below.

Children

The app does not ask for a name, age, or other personal details, and it contains no advertising or tracking. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through an optional sign-in, contact me and I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
